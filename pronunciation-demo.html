三、连读 & 弱读示范

What are you doing?
实际：Wha-da-ya doin'? — what are→"wha-da", you→"ya", doing→"doin'"
Did you get it?
实际：Di-joo ge-dit? — did you→"di-joo"(同化), get it→"ge-dit"(连读)
I'm going to have to think about it.
实际：I'm gonna hafta think about it. — going to→"gonna", have to→"hafta"
Can I get a glass of water?
实际：Ca-ni ge-da glassa water? — can I→"ca-ni", get a→"ge-da", glass of→"glassa"
Good day.
实际：Goo(d) day. — /d/ 几乎不发音，只做口型（失去爆破）
