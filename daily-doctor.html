看病就医英语：从挂号到取药，国外看病不慌张

发布于 2026-05-12  |  分类：场景口语

在异国他乡生病本来就难受，如果再描述不清症状，那个焦虑加倍。但好消息是——看病英语是高度场景化、高度公式化的。你不需要医学词汇量几千，掌握这篇的核心句就够。

第一步：电话预约挂号

你：Hi, I'd like to make an appointment to see a doctor, please.
前台：Is this for a general check-up or something urgent?
你：I've been having a bad headache and a fever for two days. I think I need to see someone soon.
前台：We have an opening at 3pm today. Can you make it?
你：Yes, 3pm works. Do I need to bring my insurance card?
前台：Yes, please bring your insurance card and ID. You can fill out the forms when you arrive.
中文英文
预约Make an appointment / Schedule an appointment
全科/家庭医生GP (General Practitioner) / Family doctor
专科医生Specialist
急诊Emergency room (ER)
保险卡Insurance card
填写表格Fill out the forms / Paperwork

第二步：描述症状——最重要的一关

这是中国英语学习者最容易卡住的地方。诀窍是：不要试图翻译中文病名，而是描述"哪里不舒服、持续多久、什么感觉"。

常用症状表达

中文英文
头疼I have a headache.
胃疼My stomach hurts. / I have a stomachache.
发烧I have a fever. / I'm running a temperature.
咳嗽I have a cough. / I've been coughing.
喉咙疼I have a sore throat.
流鼻涕I have a runny nose.
鼻塞I'm congested. / My nose is stuffed up.
头晕I feel dizzy / lightheaded.
恶心I feel nauseous. / I feel like I'm going to throw up.
腹泻I have diarrhea.
皮疹I have a rash on my [arm/neck/back].
过敏I'm allergic to [peanuts / penicillin / latex].

三个必须回答的问题

医生几乎一定会问这三个问题：

When did it start? — It started two days ago. / Since last night.
什么时候开始的？
On a scale of 1 to 10, how bad is the pain? — I'd say about a 6.
1到10分级，疼痛到什么程度？（6-7通常表示需要治疗的程度）
Are you taking any medications? — I've been taking ibuprofen. / No, nothing.
有没有在吃什么药？

第三步：看医生——听懂诊断和医嘱

医生：So what seems to be the problem today?
你：I've had a bad sore throat and a fever for two days. I've been taking some over-the-counter medicine but it's not helping much.
医生：Let me take a look. Open wide, please. … Your throat looks pretty inflamed. I'm going to run a quick strep test.
你：Is it serious?
医生：It looks like a bacterial infection. I'll prescribe some antibiotics. Take them for a full week, even if you start feeling better. Rest, drink lots of fluids, and you should be fine.
你：Should I come back for a follow-up?
医生：Only if you're not better in a week. Any allergies to medication?
你：No, none that I know of.

医生常用术语速查

医生说的意思
I'll prescribe you [药名].我给你开[药]。
Take this [次数] a day.一天吃[次数]。
Take it with food / on an empty stomach.随餐服用 / 空腹服用。
You need a blood test / an X-ray / a scan.你需要验血 / 拍X光 / 做扫描。
It's viral / bacterial.是病毒性的 / 细菌性的。
Get some rest and drink plenty of fluids.多休息多喝水（最常见的万能医嘱）。
Come back in [时间] for a follow-up.[时间]后回来复查。

第四步：取药

在国外，处方药需要去药房取，不是在医院直接拿药。药房叫 Pharmacy / Drugstore / Chemist（英国）。

你：Hi, I'm here to pick up a prescription. The name is Xiaoming.
药剂师：Let me check. … Here it is. Have you taken this before?
你：No, this is the first time.
药剂师：Okay, take one capsule twice a day with food. Don't drink alcohol while you're on this. Any questions?
你：Are there any side effects I should know about?
药剂师：Some people feel a bit drowsy. Maybe don't drive right after taking it.

总结：去诊所前就准备好的三个句子

如果你只能记三句，就记这些：

I have [症状] — it started [时间] ago.
症状 + 持续时间 = 80%的诊断依据。
I'm allergic to [过敏源].
如果你有药物过敏史，这句话必须会说。
Could you repeat that / write it down, please?
没听懂就让医生重复或写下来——这不是考试，健康比面子重要。
