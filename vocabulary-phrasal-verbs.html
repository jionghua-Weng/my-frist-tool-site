高频动词短语20个：让你的英语瞬间地道

发布于 2026-05-12  |  分类：词汇辨析

动词短语（Phrasal Verbs）是英语口语的"身份证"。英语母语者每天大量使用 run into、figure out、put off 这样的组合，而学习者往往只会用单个动词。掌握了这20个最高频的动词短语，你的口语会从"课本英语"变成"真实英语"。

为什么动词短语这么重要

对比一下你就明白：

I encountered an old friend yesterday. → 能懂，但不自然
I ran into an old friend yesterday. → 这才是日常英语

单个动词往往更正式，动词短语才是日常交流的默认选项。

第一组：最常用的6个日常短语

wake up
醒来；叫醒
I wake up at 7 every morning. / Can you wake me up at 6?
get up
起床（从床上起来）
I woke up at 7 but didn't get up until 7:30.
pick up
接（某人）；捡起；学会（语言/技能）
Can you pick me up from the airport? / I picked up some Spanish while traveling.
drop off
送（某人/东西）到某处
I'll drop you off at the station on my way to work.
hang out
一起闲逛、消磨时间
We should hang out sometime this weekend.
show up / turn up
出现、露面
He showed up 30 minutes late. / She didn't turn up at all.

第二组：沟通与理解的6个

figure out
搞懂、想明白
I can't figure out how to use this app. / Give me a minute, I'll figure it out.
find out
发现、查出来（通常是意外或主动查找后得知）
I just found out that she's leaving the company.
point out
指出
Thanks for pointing out that mistake. I hadn't noticed.
bring up
提起（话题）；抚养（孩子）
I don't want to bring it up during the meeting. / She was brought up in Shanghai.
make up
编造（故事/借口）；和好；弥补
He made up an excuse. / They had a fight but made up the next day. / I'll make it up to you.
go over
过一遍、检查
Let's go over the details one more time before we send it.

第三组：时间与进度的4个

put off
推迟、拖延
I keep putting off going to the dentist. / Don't put it off any longer.
run out (of)
用完、耗尽
We're running out of milk. / My phone battery is about to run out.
catch up (with)
赶上进度；叙旧
I need to catch up on my emails. / Let's grab coffee and catch up.
end up
最终……（结果跟一开始不一样）
We planned to go to the beach but ended up staying home.

第四组：关系与情绪变化的4个

get along (with)
相处融洽
Do you get along with your coworkers? / We get along really well.
give up
放弃
Don't give up — you're almost there. / I gave up smoking last year.
run into
偶遇、碰到
Guess who I ran into at the supermarket yesterday!
take off
脱下（衣物）；起飞；（事业）迅速发展
Take off your shoes. / The plane takes off at 6pm. / Her career really took off after that article.
记忆技巧：按核心动词分组与其死记20个独立短语，不如按核心动词分组记忆。比如 get 系列：get up（起床）、get along（相处）、get over（克服）。put 系列：put off（推迟）、put up with（忍受）、put forward（提出）。每次搞定一个动词的2-4个常见搭配，效率高得多。
